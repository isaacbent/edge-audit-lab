# edge-audit-lab

Research lab for Edge Audit (GraniteStats): synthetic trader bench + statistical pieces to tell
funded traders with a real edge from lucky ones, detect deterioration, account survival and payout risk.

Independent from the Research Engine: the few utilities it needs are copied (`edgelab/stats.py`,
`edgelab/atomic_io.py`, simplified `edgelab/registry.py`), never imported.

Rules: every study is pre-registered in `state/registry.jsonl` (append-only, hash-chained) before it runs;
a study whose code differs from its pre-registration refuses to run; failed studies are kept, never re-tuned.

## Layout
- `edgelab/simulate.py`  synthetic traders: BENCH-1 (kinds), BENCH-2 (styles), BENCH-3 (continuous edges, variable n); challenge rule
- `edgelab/stats.py`     sign-flip and t p-values, Benjamini-Hochberg, Buhlmann credibility
- `edgelab/behaviour.py` size-after-loss test (martingale / tilt)
- `edgelab/survival.py`  Kaplan-Meier, Cox proportional hazards, Harrell's concordance
- `edgelab/payout.py`    EOD account rules, monthly payouts, next-month payout forecast by day resampling
- `edgelab/drift.py`     CUSUM / EWMA edge-loss detectors with warm-up standardisation and threshold calibration
- `edgelab/registry.py`  pre-registration / results log
- `edgelab/study.py`     code lock, seed standard errors / paired differences, --smoke helper
- `tools/skill_power.py`, `skill_rank.py`, `skill_rank_v2.py`, `skill_rank_v3.py`, `skill_diag.py`, `skill_calib_b3.py`, `skill_mintrades.py`  SKILL-01..07 studies; `tools/drift_detect.py` DRIFT-01; `tools/tilt_detect.py` TILT-01; `tools/survival_accounts.py` SURVIVAL-01; `tools/payout_book.py`, `payout_book_v2.py` PAYOUT-01/02
- `tests/`               `python3 -m pytest -q`

| SURVIVAL-01 | 2026-10-29 | FAILED narrowly (BENCH-3S: funded accounts over 90 days, 10 % static / 5 % daily loss limits, staggered starts and voluntary exits, 25 % censored). | PAYOUT-01 | 2026-10-29 | FAILED badly (BENCH-3P: 1000-account books, shared daily market move; 200 books). 90 % interval for next month's total payouts from month 1: coverage 0.23 [0.17, 0.29] with common-day resampling (criterion 0.85-0.95), 0.04 with independent accounts; realised total below the interval in 78 % / 94 % of books; with no common market (control) coverage 0. Cause: survivorship bias, not the correlation. Accounts alive after month 1 earned +0.08 % a day in month 1 and lose -0.10 % a day in month 2 (most have no real edge), so replaying their month-1 days doubles the forecast (about 2000 vs 960 realised, dev seeds 9600-9602). Lesson for the product: never forecast payouts from survivors' raw past P&L. |
| PAYOUT-02 | 2026-10-29 | FAILED (same bench and criterion as PAYOUT-01, new books). Survivorship fix (each account's days re-centred on its NPMLE posterior mean, prior on all accounts incl. breached): coverage 0.77 [0.71, 0.82] at corr 0.2 (criterion 0.85-0.95; PAYOUT-01 method 0.26 on the same books). The bias is gone: misses are now balanced (10 % below, 13 % above, PIT roughly flat with heavier ends), the interval is just too narrow when the market is shared. Without a shared market coverage is 0.99 (too wide). Remaining miss = the month's market drift, which one month of book data cannot separate from the population's average edge. |
